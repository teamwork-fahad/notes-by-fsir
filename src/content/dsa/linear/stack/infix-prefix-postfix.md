## Infix, prefix, and postfix

For the expression `A + B * C`:

- Infix: `A + B * C`
- Prefix: `+ A * B C`
- Postfix: `A B C * +`

A stack can convert infix to postfix by applying operator precedence and can evaluate postfix expressions by pushing operands and applying operators.
