## What is Mean?

**Mean is the average value of a dataset.** It is one of the most common measures of central tendency.

In simple words:

> **Mean gives us one value that represents the average of all values in the dataset.**

## Why do we use Mean?

Suppose the marks of 5 students are **40, 50, 60, 70, 80**. If we want to know the overall average of the students' marks, Mean is useful. Instead of looking at all five values, we can say:

> **The average mark is 60.**

Mean helps us summarize data with one value.

## How do we calculate Mean?

There are three basic steps:

1. Add all the values.
2. Count the values.
3. Divide the sum by the number of values.

### Example

Data: **10, 20, 30, 40, 50**

$$
10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50 = 150
$$

There are **5 values**.

$$
\text{Mean} = \frac{150}{5} = 30
$$

Therefore, **Mean = 30**.

## Formula of Mean

$$
\text{Mean} = \frac{\text{Sum of all values}}{\text{Number of values}}
$$

In statistics, we commonly write:

$$
\boxed{\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x}{n}}
$$

Here, $\bar{x}$ is the mean, $x$ is each value, $\sum x$ is the sum of all values, and $n$ is the number of values.

So basically:

> **Mean = Total of values divided by the number of values.**

## Important point

Mean uses **every value** in the dataset. Therefore, every observation contributes to the Mean.

## Effect of Extreme Values

Mean can be strongly affected by very large or very small values, called **outliers**.

For the data **10, 20, 30, 40, 50**:

$$
\text{Mean} = \frac{150}{5} = 30
$$

If 50 becomes 500, the new data is **10, 20, 30, 40, 500**:

$$
\text{Mean} = \frac{600}{5} = 120
$$

One very large value changed the Mean from **30 to 120**.

## Remember

- Mean gives the average value.
- Mean uses all values in the dataset.
- Mean is easy to calculate.
- Mean can be affected by very large or very small values.
