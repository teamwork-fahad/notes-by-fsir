# Unit 1: Introduction to Computers and its Components

## 1.1 Computer

### 1.1.1 Introduction to Computer
Computers are electronic devices that process data and information. They are essential tools in modern society used for computation, communication, and data management.

### 1.1.2 The Components of Computer
A computer consists of:
- **Hardware**: Physical components (CPU, RAM, Storage, etc.)
- **Software**: Programs and operating systems
- **Peripherals**: Input/Output devices

### 1.1.3 Advantages and Disadvantages of Computer

**Advantages:**
- High speed processing
- Accuracy and reliability
- Large storage capacity
- Versatility
- Cost-effective

**Disadvantages:**
- High initial cost
- Requires trained personnel
- Dependency on electricity
- Data security concerns

### 1.1.4 Generations of Computer
- **First Generation (1946-1956)**: Vacuum tubes
- **Second Generation (1956-1963)**: Transistors
- **Third Generation (1964-1971)**: Integrated circuits
- **Fourth Generation (1971-1985)**: Microprocessors
- **Fifth Generation (1985-present)**: Artificial Intelligence

### 1.1.5 Computer Software
- **System Software**: Operating systems, device drivers
- **Application Software**: Word processors, browsers, games

### 1.1.6 Categories of Computers

#### 1.1.6.1 Personal Computers
Desktop and laptop computers for individual use.

#### 1.1.6.2 Mobile Computers
Tablets, smartphones, and portable devices.

#### 1.1.6.3 Servers
Computers that provide services to other computers over a network.

#### 1.1.6.4 Mainframes
Large computers for processing vast amounts of data in enterprises.

#### 1.1.6.5 Super Computers
Most powerful computers used for complex calculations and simulations.

#### 1.1.6.6 ATM
Automated Teller Machines for banking operations.

#### 1.1.6.7 POS
Point of Sale systems used in retail stores.

### 1.1.7 Usage and Applications of Computer in Society
- **Education**: E-learning and research
- **Healthcare**: Medical diagnosis and patient records
- **Business**: Financial management and operations
- **Entertainment**: Gaming, streaming, social media
- **Government**: Administrative and public services
