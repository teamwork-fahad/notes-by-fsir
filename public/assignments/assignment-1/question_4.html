📌 Question 4: Calculate Coefficient of Variation (C.V.)
Problem Statement: Calculate the co-efficient of variation (C.V.) from the following data:
Age (in years)0–910–1920–2930–3940–4950–59
No. of persons ($f$)13386776224
💡 Easy Guide / आसान तरीका:
- Inclusive class intervals (0-9, 10-19) ke mid-values hote hain: $\frac{0+9}{2} = 4.5, 14.5, 24.5 \dots$
- Step-deviation method se calculation easy ho jaati hai: $A = 24.5, h = 10, u_i = \frac{x_i - 24.5}{10}$.
- Formula: $C.V. = \frac{\sigma}{\bar{x}} \times 100$.
1 Step 1: Step-Deviation Calculation Table

Let Assumed Mean $A = 24.5$, width $h = 10$. $u_i = \frac{x_i - 24.5}{10}$.

Age (Class) $f_i$ Mid-point ($x_i$) $u_i$ $f_i u_i$ $u_i^2$ $f_i u_i^2$
0–9134.5-2-26452
10–193814.5-1-38138
20–296724.50000

Total $N = 220$ — — $\sum f_i u_i = 68$ — $\sum f_i u_i^2 = 290$
2 Step 2: Calculate Mean ($\bar{x}$)
$$\bar{x} = A + \left( \frac{\sum f_i u_i}{N} \right) \times h$$ $$\bar{x} = 24.5 + \left( \frac{68}{220} \right) \times 10 = 24.5 + 3.0909 = \mathbf{27.5909 \text{ years}}$$
3 Step 3: Calculate Standard Deviation ($\sigma$)
$$\sigma = h \times \sqrt{ \frac{\sum f_i u_i^2}{N} - \left( \frac{\sum f_i u_i}{N} \right)^2 }$$ $$\frac{\sum f_i u_i^2}{N} = \frac{290}{220} \approx 1.318182$$ $$\left( \frac{\sum f_i u_i}{N} \right)^2 = \left( \frac{68}{220} \right)^2 = (0.309091)^2 \approx 0.095537$$ $$\text{Variance in } u = 1.318182 - 0.095537 = 1.222645$$ $$\sigma = 10 \times \sqrt{1.222645} = 10 \times 1.105733 = \mathbf{11.0573 \text{ years}}$$
4 Step 4: Calculate Coefficient of Variation (C.V.)
$$C.V. = \frac{\sigma}{\bar{x}} \times 100$$ $$C.V. = \frac{11.0573}{27.5909} \times 100 \approx \mathbf{40.08\%}$$

✅ Final Answer:

  • Mean ($\bar{x}$) = 27.59 years
  • Standard Deviation ($\sigma$) = 11.06 years
  • Coefficient of Variation (C.V.) = 40.08%
