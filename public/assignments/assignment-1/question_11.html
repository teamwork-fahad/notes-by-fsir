📌 Question 11: Find Missing Frequencies f₁ and f₂
Problem Statement: Find the missing frequencies $f_1$ and $f_2$ for the following data whose mean is 1.46 and total frequency is 200:
$x_i$012345
$f_i$46$f_1$$f_2$25105
💡 Easy Guide / आसान तरीका:
- Yeh discrete series hai ($x_i$ direct values hain).
- Total frequency $N = 200 \implies f_1 + f_2 = 114$.
- Mean $\bar{x} = 1.46 \implies \sum f_i x_i = 200 \times 1.46 = 292$.
- Dono linear equations ko subtract karke $f_1$ aur $f_2$ aasani se mil jate hain.
1 Step 1: Construct Calculation Table
$x_i$$f_i$$f_i x_i$
046$46 \times 0 = 0$
1$f_1$$f_1 \times 1 = f_1$
2$f_2$$f_2 \times 2 = 2f_2$
325$25 \times 3 = 75$
410$10 \times 4 = 40$
55$5 \times 5 = 25$
Total $N = 200$ $\sum f_i x_i = 140 + f_1 + 2f_2$
2 Step 2: Form Equation (1) from Total Frequency
$$46 + f_1 + f_2 + 25 + 10 + 5 = 200$$ $$86 + f_1 + f_2 = 200$$ $$f_1 + f_2 = 114 \quad \text{--- [Equation 1]}$$
3 Step 3: Form Equation (2) from Mean (1.46)
$$\bar{x} = \frac{\sum f_i x_i}{N} = 1.46$$ $$\frac{140 + f_1 + 2f_2}{200} = 1.46$$ $$140 + f_1 + 2f_2 = 200 \times 1.46 = 292$$ $$f_1 + 2f_2 = 292 - 140 = 152 \quad \text{--- [Equation 2]}$$
4 Step 4: Solve Simultaneous Equations

Subtract Equation 1 from Equation 2:

$$(f_1 + 2f_2) - (f_1 + f_2) = 152 - 114$$ $$f_2 = \mathbf{38}$$

Substitute $f_2 = 38$ into Equation 1:

$$f_1 + 38 = 114 \implies f_1 = 114 - 38 = \mathbf{76}$$

✅ Final Answer:

  • $f_1 = \mathbf{76}$
  • $f_2 = \mathbf{38}$
