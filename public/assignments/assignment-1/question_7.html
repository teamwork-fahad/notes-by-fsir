📌 Question 7: Mean Deviation about Mean and Median
Problem Statement: Find the mean deviation about mean and mean deviation about median for the following data:
Data values: $3, 9, 5, 3, 12, 10, 18, 4, 7, 19, 21$
💡 Easy Guide / आसान तरीका:
- Total items $n = 11$.
- Part 1: Sum nikalo $\sum x = 111$, Mean = $\frac{111}{11} = 10.0909$. Fir har value ka $|x - \bar{x}|$ nikaal kar average lo.
- Part 2: Values ko ascending order me arrange karo. Middle value (6th term) Median hogi = $9$. Fir $|x - 9|$ nikaal kar average lo.
1 Part A: Mean Deviation about Mean

$$\sum x_i = 3 + 9 + 5 + 3 + 12 + 10 + 18 + 4 + 7 + 19 + 21 = 111$$

$$\bar{x} = \frac{111}{11} \approx 10.0909$$

$$MD(\bar{x}) = \frac{\sum |x_i - \bar{x}|}{n} = \frac{59.2727}{11} \approx \mathbf{5.3884}$$
2 Part B: Mean Deviation about Median

Arranging data in ascending order: $$3, 3, 4, 5, 7, \mathbf{9}, 10, 12, 18, 19, 21$$

Since $n = 11$ (odd): $$\text{Median } (M) = \left(\frac{11+1}{2}\right)\text{-th term} = 6\text{-th term} = \mathbf{9}$$

✅ Final Answer:

  • Mean = 10.09
  • Mean Deviation about Mean = 5.39
  • Median = 9
  • Mean Deviation about Median = 5.27 (or $\frac{58}{11}$)
