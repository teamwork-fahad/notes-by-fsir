📌 Question 9: Find D₆, P₂₀ and P₁₀₀
Problem Statement: Find $D_6$, $P_{20}$ and $P_{100}$ from the following data:
Class0–1010–2020–3030–4040–5050–60
Frequency5821854
💡 Easy Guide / आसान तरीका:
- Total frequency $N = 51$.
- $D_6$ position = $\frac{6 \times 51}{10} = 30.6$.
- $P_{20}$ position = $\frac{20 \times 51}{100} = 10.2$.
- $P_{100}$ position = $51$ (jo distribution ki upper limit $60$ ko represent karta hai).

2 Step 2: Calculate 6th Decile ($D_6$)

Position: $\frac{6N}{10} = 30.6 \implies$ Class 20–30 ($L = 20, cf = 13, f = 21, h = 10$).

$$D_6 = 20 + \left( \frac{30.6 - 13}{21} \right) \times 10 = 20 + \left( \frac{17.6}{21} \right) \times 10 = 20 + \frac{176}{21} \approx \mathbf{28.3810}$$
3 Step 3: Calculate 20th Percentile ($P_{20}$)

Position: $\frac{20N}{100} = 10.2 \implies$ Class 10–20 ($L = 10, cf = 5, f = 8, h = 10$).

$$P_{20} = 10 + \left( \frac{10.2 - 5}{8} \right) \times 10 = 10 + \left( \frac{5.2}{8} \right) \times 10 = 10 + 6.5 = \mathbf{16.5}$$
4 Step 4: Calculate 100th Percentile ($P_{100}$)

Position: $\frac{100N}{100} = 51 \implies$ Highest Class 50–60 ($L = 50, cf = 47, f = 4, h = 10$).

$$P_{100} = 50 + \left( \frac{51 - 47}{4} \right) \times 10 = 50 + \left( \frac{4}{4} \right) \times 10 = 50 + 10 = \mathbf{60}$$

(Note: 100th percentile is the maximum boundary value of the distribution = 60)

✅ Final Answer:

  • $D_6$ = 28.38
  • $P_{20}$ = 16.5
  • $P_{100}$ = 60
