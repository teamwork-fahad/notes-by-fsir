📌 Question 2: Find D₅ and D₉
Problem Statement: Find $D_5$ and $D_9$ for the following data:
Class0–44–88–1212–1616–2020–2424–2828–32
Frequency1012875846
💡 Easy Guide / आसान तरीका:
Deciles data ko 10 equal parts me divide karte hain.
$D_k$ ka formula: $L + \left(\frac{\frac{k \cdot N}{10} - cf}{f}\right) \times h$.
$D_5$ ke liye position $\frac{5N}{10}$ aur $D_9$ ke liye $\frac{9N}{10}$ nikalte hain.

2 Step 2: Calculate 5th Decile ($D_5$)

1. Position of $D_5$: $\frac{5N}{10} = \frac{5 \times 60}{10} = 30$.

2. Looking at the $cf$ column, $30$ lies exactly at the end of class 8–12.

  • Lower limit ($L$) = $8$
  • Cumulative frequency of preceding class ($cf$) = $22$
  • Frequency of class ($f$) = $8$
  • Class width ($h$) = $4$
$$D_5 = L + \left( \frac{\frac{5N}{10} - cf}{f} \right) \times h$$ $$D_5 = 8 + \left( \frac{30 - 22}{8} \right) \times 4 = 8 + \left( \frac{8}{8} \right) \times 4 = 8 + 4 = \mathbf{12}$$
3 Step 3: Calculate 9th Decile ($D_9$)

1. Position of $D_9$: $\frac{9N}{10} = \frac{9 \times 60}{10} = 54$.

2. In the $cf$ column, $54$ lies in the class 24–28.

  • Lower limit ($L$) = $24$
  • $cf$ of previous class ($cf$) = $50$
  • Frequency of class ($f$) = $4$
  • Class width ($h$) = $4$
$$D_9 = L + \left( \frac{\frac{9N}{10} - cf}{f} \right) \times h$$ $$D_9 = 24 + \left( \frac{54 - 50}{4} \right) \times 4 = 24 + \left( \frac{4}{4} \right) \times 4 = 24 + 4 = \mathbf{28}$$

✅ Final Answer:

  • 5th Decile ($D_5$) = 12
  • 9th Decile ($D_9$) = 28
