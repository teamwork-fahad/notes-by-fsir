$$D_8 = 50 + \left( \frac{482.4 - 443}{65} \right) \times 10 = 50 + \left( \frac{39.4}{65} \right) \times 10 = 50 + 6.0615 = \mathbf{56.0615}$$

$$P_{65} = 40 + \left( \frac{391.95 - 236}{207} \right) \times 10 = 40 + \left( \frac{155.95}{207} \right) \times 10 = 40 + 7.5338 = \mathbf{47.5338}$$
