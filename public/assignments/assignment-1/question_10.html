📌 Question 10: Find Missing Frequencies f₁ and f₂
Problem Statement: Find the missing frequencies $f_1$ and $f_2$ of the following distribution, given that the mean is 21.4 and total frequency is 40:
Class-Interval0–88–1616–2424–3232–40
Frequency6$f_1$10$f_2$9
💡 Easy Guide / आसान तरीका:
Yahan 2 unknowns ($f_1$ aur $f_2$) hain, isliye 2 equations banengi:
- Equation 1: Total frequency = $40 \implies f_1 + f_2 = 15$.
- Equation 2: Mean = $21.4 \implies \frac{\sum f_i x_i}{40} = 21.4$.
Dono ko solve karke exact integer answers mil jaate hain!
1 Step 1: Construct Calculation Table with Mid-values
Class Interval Frequency ($f_i$) Mid-point ($x_i$) $f_i x_i$
0–864$6 \times 4 = 24$
8–16$f_1$12$12 f_1$
16–241020$10 \times 20 = 200$

Total $N = 40$ — $\sum f_i x_i = 548 + 12f_1 + 28f_2$
2 Step 2: Form Equation (1) from Total Frequency
$$6 + f_1 + 10 + f_2 + 9 = 40$$ $$25 + f_1 + f_2 = 40$$ $$f_1 + f_2 = 15 \quad \text{--- [Equation 1]}$$
3 Step 3: Form Equation (2) from Given Mean (21.4)
$$\bar{x} = \frac{\sum f_i x_i}{N} = 21.4$$ $$\frac{548 + 12f_1 + 28f_2}{40} = 21.4$$ $$548 + 12f_1 + 28f_2 = 40 \times 21.4 = 856$$ $$12f_1 + 28f_2 = 856 - 548 = 308$$ Divide entire equation by $4$: $$3f_1 + 7f_2 = 77 \quad \text{--- [Equation 2]}$$
4 Step 4: Solve the Equations Simultaneously

From Equation 1: $f_1 = 15 - f_2$. Substitute into Equation 2:

$$3(15 - f_2) + 7f_2 = 77$$ $$45 - 3f_2 + 7f_2 = 77$$ $$4f_2 = 77 - 45 = 32$$ $$f_2 = \frac{32}{4} = \mathbf{8}$$

Now calculate $f_1$:

$$f_1 = 15 - f_2 = 15 - 8 = \mathbf{7}$$

✅ Final Answer:

  • $f_1 = \mathbf{7}$
  • $f_2 = \mathbf{8}$
